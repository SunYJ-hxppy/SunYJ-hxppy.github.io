I am a Third-year Ph.D. student at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Seong Jae Hwang, in the Medical Imaging and Computer Vision (MICV).

My doctoral research explores the capabilities of generative models within the domain of computer vision. I am actively working on problems in video and image generation, motion transfer in video generation, and diffusion models.

I am currently looking for a Research Internship position! Please feel free to contact me!
