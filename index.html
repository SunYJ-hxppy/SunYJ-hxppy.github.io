Sunyoung Jung

I am a third-year Ph.D. student at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Seong Jae Hwang, in the Medical Imaging and Computer Vision (MICV) lab. My doctoral research explores the capabilities of generative models within the domain of computer vision. I am actively working on problems in video generation, controllable video generation, and diffusion- and flow-based generative models, with a growing interest in world models and egocentric video modeling.

I'd love to connect with people who share similar interests! Feel free to reach out via email!
